Gold Gear Awards

President's List and Dean's List. Per the BulSU Student Handbook, the award is based on the grade point average for the last two consecutive semesters (2nd semester of the preceding academic year and 1st semester of the current one), with no grade lower than 2.0. The combined GWA follows the sample computation for the Gold Gear Awards: (Semester 1 GWA × units) + (Semester 2 GWA × units), divided by the total units of both semesters. Each semester's GWA is also shown.

Latin Honors

Enter subjects from every completed semester. Cumulative GWA and the lowest-performing individual grade are evaluated separately for each honor. Each honor needs both its GWA limit and its individual-grade limit. A GWA better than an honor's band still meets that honor's GWA requirement, so if a higher honor fails its grade limit, the next honor is checked on its own. A higher honor is never awarded on GWA alone: if your GWA is better than an honor's range but your lowest grade fails that honor's limit, the next honor down is assessed.
